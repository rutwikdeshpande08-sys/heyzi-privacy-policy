Privacy Policy for Heyzi

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Welcome to Heyzi – AI Reply Assistant ("Heyzi", "we", "our" or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may process the following information:

  • Account information: Information associated with your Google account, such as your email address, name and Firebase user identifier, when you sign in using Google.
  • Device and account identifiers: Identifiers used to recognise your app installation, maintain your account and manage your credits.
  • Messages you submit: Text that you paste or share with Heyzi for AI-generated reply suggestions.
  • Credit and purchase information: Credit balances, credit transactions and relevant subscription or purchase status.
  • Advertising information: Information that may be collected by advertising services when advertisements are displayed.
  • Technical information: Information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the application.

2. How We Use Information

We use information for purposes including:

  • Creating and maintaining user accounts.
  • Providing AI-generated reply suggestions.
  • Managing credits and purchases.
  • Providing subscription features.
  • Displaying advertisements where applicable.
  • Maintaining application security and preventing misuse.
  • Responding to support and privacy requests.

3. AI Message Processing

When you submit text to generate reply suggestions, that text is transmitted to Heyzi's backend service and processed using Google's Gemini AI service to generate responses.

Please avoid submitting passwords, financial information, sensitive personal information or other information you do not want processed by an AI service.

4. Third-Party Services

Heyzi uses or may use the following third-party services to provide its features:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: For Google account sign-in and authentication.
  • Google Gemini: For AI-powered reply generation.
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements, where enabled.
  • Google Play Billing: For processing in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • Backend and database hosting providers: To operate Heyzi's services and maintain account and credit information.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and service terms.

5. Advertising

Heyzi may display advertisements using Google AdMob. Advertising providers may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address and app interactions to provide, measure and improve advertisements, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at Google's Privacy Policy .

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled through Google Play Billing. Payment card and payment account details are handled by Google Play. Heyzi may receive purchase-related information needed to verify purchases and provide credits or subscription benefits.

7. Data Retention and Security

We retain account, credit and transaction information for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain account records, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations.

Information submitted for AI generation is processed to provide the requested service. Technical logs and information handled by our service providers may be subject to their respective retention practices.

We use reasonable measures designed to protect information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Heyzi account and associated personal information by contacting us at the email address below.

We will verify the request and process deletion subject to applicable legal, security and transaction-record retention requirements.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or necessary to resolve outstanding transactions, prevent fraud or protect our legitimate interests.

9. Children's Privacy

Heyzi is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the request.

10. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to ask about the personal information associated with your account, request corrections where applicable, or request account and data deletion.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
